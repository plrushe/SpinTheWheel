Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Football Roulette is a football lineup randomiser. You can use it without an account and without accepting optional storage. This policy explains the current website, the information you choose to enter and the controls available to you.

Who to contact

The site owner operates Football Roulette. For privacy questions, requests about your information or other enquiries, contact plrushe@outlook.com. Please avoid entering sensitive information into player or team names; nicknames work just as well.

Player names and lineups

Player names, team names, position assignments and set-piece duties are processed in your browser to provide the randomiser. This application does not send them to an application server or sell them. Without optional-storage permission, names and settings are held for the current page session and are lost when it is closed or reloaded.

If you download a lineup, the PNG is generated on your device. You control whether you save or share it. The website cannot delete copies you have downloaded or shared.

Cookies and similar storage

The application currently sets no HTTP cookies and loads no advertising or analytics trackers. It uses browser local storage, which is also a storage technology covered by this policy.

No optional categories are preselected. Rejecting optional storage does not block the game. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings. Withdrawing permission removes the saved game preferences; your current in-memory lineup remains until you leave the page. The privacy decision itself is retained so we can honour it.

“Clear saved roster & settings” in Cookie settings deletes the application’s saved names and team preference while retaining the privacy choice. You can also clear all site data in your browser, which removes the privacy choice too. Using a shared device? Clear saved data before you leave.

Hosting and contacting us

Your browser necessarily sends connection information, such as your IP address, requested URL and browser information, to the hosting provider to retrieve the website. The provider may process access and security logs to deliver and protect the service. Its retention, location and any international transfers depend on the hosting arrangement. Contact the site owner for details about the deployed service.

If you email us, your email address and message are handled to respond to your enquiry and manage any follow-up. Email involves the email service provider. Please send only the information needed for your request. Correspondence is kept only as needed for the enquiry and any applicable legal obligations.

Advertising and Google services

This release does not load Google AdSense or analytics scripts and does not currently share game data with Google for advertising. The storage notice on this site is not a Google-certified advertising consent platform.

If advertising is introduced, this policy and the consent interface must first be updated for the actual providers and processing. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses or other identifiers to deliver, measure and personalise ads. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform must obtain the relevant choices before advertising technologies load. Rejecting or withdrawing advertising consent must remain possible.

Read how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Your rights and legal bases

Optional storage relies on your consent, which you can withdraw as described above. Processing needed to deliver the service or respond to an enquiry relies on the applicable service-related basis; proportionate hosting and security processing may rely on legitimate interests. Any legal obligations apply where relevant.

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of personal information, object to processing, obtain portable data and withdraw consent. Contact us using the email above. Browser-only data can usually be deleted immediately using Cookie settings or your browser’s site-data controls.

You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. We do not intentionally collect children’s information or operate accounts for children.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the service or its data practices change. Material changes to optional storage or tracking require a fresh choice before those technologies are used.
